# ✍️ harper - Fix your writing with privacy first

[![Download Harper](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Blue-blue)](https://github.com/Caryleastern298/harper)

Harper improves your writing. It checks your grammar and spelling without sending data to a server. You keep your work private. The software runs on your computer. It uses Rust to reach high speeds.

You need a computer running Windows 10 or Windows 11 to use this app. Harper does not need an internet connection. It works on your machine alone. 

Follow these steps to install the program:

1. Visit this page to download: [https://github.com/Caryleastern298/harper](https://github.com/Caryleastern298/harper)
2. Look for the file labeled for Windows.
3. Save the file to your desktop.
4. Open the file to start the installation.
5. Follow the prompts on your screen.

## 🛠️ How to use Harper

Open the app after it finishes installing. A small window appears. You can type directly into the box or paste text from another file. Harper finds errors in real time.

It highlights mistakes in your sentences. Hover your mouse over a highlighted word to see a suggestion. Click the suggestion to replace your incorrect word. 

Harper scans for these items:
- Spelling errors
- Missing punctuation
- Word choice issues
- Sentence structure problems

## 🔒 Your Privacy

Most grammar tools send your text to the cloud. They store your words on third-party servers. Harper does things differently. It processes your text locally. Nothing leaves your laptop. You control your information. No one sees your drafts except you.

## 🚀 Performance

Harper uses modern technology to stay fast. It ignores heavy web frameworks that cause lag. The engine uses Rust for efficiency. It checks long documents in milliseconds. You notice no slowdown while you type.

## ⚙️ Settings

You can change how Harper works. Open the settings menu to view options.

- Language: Set your preferred English dialect.
- Sensitivity: Choose how many suggestions you want to see.
- Ignore List: Add words that Harper should skip.
- Auto-run: Decide if the app starts when you turn on your computer.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

The app should work on any standard Windows PC. If you see an error, check these items:

Check your disk space. You need at least 500 megabytes of storage to run Harper.
Update your Windows version if the app fails to open.
Restart your computer. This clears minor conflicts.

If the app feels slow, close other programs. Harper uses your processor to find errors. Too many apps open at once can reduce speed.
